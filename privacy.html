Lesik Receipt Automation - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Lesik Receipt Automation is an internal business application operated by Lesik Electrical Services LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when it connects to QuickBooks Online and related business accounting workflows.

2. Information We Access

When authorized by an account owner or authorized user, the application may access information available through QuickBooks Online, including business accounting information such as customers, vendors, accounts, transactions, receipts, expenses, invoices, dates, amounts, and related accounting information.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through QuickBooks Online is used solely to support internal business accounting workflows, including receipt processing, categorization, transaction matching, expense tracking, and reconciliation.

4. QuickBooks Online

Lesik Receipt Automation uses Intuit's QuickBooks Online services. Access to QuickBooks Online information occurs only after the user authorizes the application through Intuit's authorization process.

5. Information Sharing

Lesik Receipt Automation does not sell personal information or QuickBooks Online accounting information.

Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide, maintain, secure, or operate the application and its authorized business workflows, or when required by law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect information accessed by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to perform the application's intended business functions, maintain accounting records, comply with legal requirements, or resolve disputes.

8. User Control and Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their QuickBooks Online account through the applicable Intuit account or authorization controls. Revoking authorization prevents the application from obtaining additional information through that authorization.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, business practices, or applicable requirements. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
