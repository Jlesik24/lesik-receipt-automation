1. About This Page

League Operator is a private tool that one operator uses to publish posts to two Facebook Pages:

  • Skyview Amusements Pool Leagues
  • Last Rack Billiards

The app has no public application interface or public sign-up. Only the operator authorizes Facebook access, through a manual setup process.

The app does not specifically request Facebook user profiles, visitor identities, follower lists, comments or reactions. Page post text may contain people's names, and league records used to prepare posts may also contain personal information.

Send requests directly to the operator. There is no form on this page or automated deletion system. The operator can manually remove or edit Facebook posts, delete individual messages from the private Telegram chat, and reply by email. Removal of stored database text is currently unavailable. Sections 4 and 6 explain the current capabilities and remaining limitations.

2. What Data Exists

  • Posts on the Facebook Pages.
  • On the operator's computer, for each post: a delivery log entry, until eligible cleanup (Page, time, status, Facebook post ID, a fingerprint of the content, and the first 120 characters of the text) with delivery diagnostics; and an approval record (identifiers, fingerprints, the outcome, the Facebook post ID and any remaining preview of the text) with its history and a short excerpt in the software's internal work list.
  • On the operator's computer: drafts waiting for approval, and for weekly league result posts the full text of each version of the post.
  • On the operator's computer: any image file used in a post (no post has included an image to date).
  • In the operator's private Telegram chat with the software: the draft and approval messages for a post, and notices about a published post, which include its Facebook post ID.
  • On the operator's computer, for each post: the record of the logged-out visibility check (Page ID, post ID, a copy of the post text, the check results and times).
  • On the operator's computer: the ID and name of each Page and one Page access token for each Page.
  • Backup copies of the items held on the operator's computer (see section 6).
  • If you make a request: your message and contact details in the operator's email mailbox, and, once approved and in use, a request log.

3. Types of Request and What to Include

Send your request to: lesikes@outlook.com

  • Content request (correct or remove information about you): identify the Page and post if available, or describe the draft, image or information concerned.
  • Access-removal request (remove the app's access to a Page): identify the Page and your role administering it.
  • Other request: describe what you are asking for.

Say how the operator can reply to you. The operator may ask for enough information to confirm that the request relates to you or to a Page you administer. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

Skyview Pool Leagues is open only to players aged 21 and over. Last Rack Billiards tournaments and events are open to players of all ages, including players under 18, so results posted to the Last Rack Billiards Page may name participants who are under 18. A participant, or a parent or guardian of a participant under 18, may make a request.

The operator will assess your request and report what can currently be done, what was completed and what remains pending.

4. Current Handling and Limitations

For a content request about a published post

  • Requests are received in the operator's email mailbox. No request log is currently in use.
  • The operator will remove or edit the post by hand using Facebook's Page controls and will report what was done and what was observed afterwards. The software does not delete or edit Facebook posts.
  • The operator cannot currently remove stored post text from the software's database. Removal of delivery previews, approval previews and work-list excerpts, weekly post text, and visibility-check text and notes depends on approval and readiness of the deletion procedure and supporting tools. The operator will identify remaining copies and explain what remains pending.
  • The operator will delete the draft and approval messages for the post from the operator's private Telegram chat by hand and will report anything that could not be deleted.
  • Removing a Facebook post or Telegram message does not remove the software's stored text, approval records, delivery records or visibility-check records. These currently remain stored; backup copies are described in section 6.
  • Removing a past post does not by itself prevent a name from appearing in a later post, because posts are prepared from league records. Say so in your request if that is also what you are asking for; the operator will reply with what can be done.
  • Backup copies are addressed separately in section 6.

For a content request about a draft or an image

  • The operator can decline a draft that is waiting for approval, so that it is not published, and delete the related messages from the operator's private Telegram chat by hand. No post has carried an image and photo publishing is not currently available through the software. The operator will reply with what was done and what remains stored.

For an access-removal request

  • The operator will request removal of the app's authorization through Facebook and will report what was verified. Both Pages were connected under one authorization, so this may affect both Pages. If the operator stops using the app with a Page, the app's access is removed, the User requests deletion of information obtained from Facebook, or Meta requires its deletion (which for this app includes the two Page access tokens, the Page IDs and names, the Facebook post IDs, and delivery-status and visibility-check details obtained from Facebook), the operator will promptly end the app's access to the affected Page(s) on Facebook, which is intended to make the stored Page access tokens unusable, and will promptly delete the Page access tokens, Page IDs and Page names from the software's active configuration and from local copies of that configuration. The remaining copies - Page IDs, Facebook post IDs and those details held in the software's database and in notices in the operator's private Telegram chat, copies of them in local safety copies of the database, and copies of the configuration and the database in off-site backups - are not removed by those steps; the operator removes them in a separate step as soon as reasonably possible and does not use them after access is removed. Deleting the Telegram notices is available by hand today. Removal from the database, its copies and off-site backups requires supporting tools that have been tested on synthetic data; that work has not yet been rehearsed or verified in production. Section 6 describes those copies. The 30-calendar-day completion target does not limit or delay this paragraph.

For any other request

  • The operator will reply to explain what applies and what will be done. Requests about other records the business holds, such as league records, payment records or receipts, are handled separately by the operator, who will reply with what applies to each kind of record. The steps on this page do not change those records.

5. Timing and Confirmation

The operator will acknowledge your request within 3 calendar days of receipt. The completion target is 30 calendar days from receipt, subject to necessary verification and applicable legal obligations. Database removal is currently unavailable and depends on approval and readiness of the deletion procedure and supporting tools, so the operator cannot currently assure completion of requests requiring that work within the target. If work remains pending at 30 calendar days, the operator will report what has been completed, what remains pending and why, and when remaining work will next be reviewed.

The backup copies described in section 6 are not part of the applicable steps: this procedure does not remove information from them, except as stated below for information obtained from Facebook.

Deleting the Telegram notices is available by hand today. Removal from the database, its copies and off-site backups requires supporting tools that have been tested on synthetic data; that work has not yet been rehearsed or verified in production. The reply will say what was changed in the software's records, what was kept and why, what the operator did on Facebook and in Telegram, anything that could not be done, and what remains in backup copies.

6. Backup Copies

Except as stated in sections 4 and 5 for information obtained from Facebook, this procedure does not remove individual records from existing backup snapshots. Backup copies made before a removal, and later backups containing older local safety copies, may retain the removed information. For information other than information obtained from Facebook, no date of erasure from backup storage is promised. Information obtained from Facebook remains subject to the removal commitment in sections 4 and 5.

  • The operator's computer is configured to be backed up nightly to encrypted off-site storage. Backup snapshot selection uses 7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly retention settings. These settings do not guarantee erasure within six months.
  • Data from removed snapshots remains in storage until a separate cleanup is run; that cleanup has not yet been run.
  • Local safety copies are ordinary copies of database and configuration files kept on the operator's computer. They have no scheduled deletion, are included in subsequent off-site backups, and are not changed by this procedure, except as stated in sections 4 and 5 for information obtained from Facebook.

7. Copies Outside the Operator's Control

The operator cannot control Meta's retention of internal copies or independently retained screenshots, copies or caches of a public post. The operator's reply describes what the operator did and observed; it is not a confirmation of deletion by Meta or by Telegram.

8. Removing the App's Access Yourself

A person who authorized an app with their own Facebook account can remove it in their Facebook account settings, in the section that lists the apps and business integrations connected to the account. This description has not been checked against Facebook's current menus; Facebook's Help Center describes the current steps.

Only the operator has authorized this app. If you administer one of the Pages and want the app's access removed, send an access-removal request as described in section 3.

Removing an app's access does not by itself delete posts that were already published or the records described in section 2.
