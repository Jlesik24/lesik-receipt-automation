Lesik Receipt Automation - Terms of Use

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

1. Purpose

Lesik Receipt Automation is an internal business application used by Lesik Electrical Services LLC to process business receipts and assist with accounting workflows.

2. Use of the Application

The application may process receipt information, including vendor names, transaction dates, invoice or order numbers, purchased items, quantities, prices, totals, and associated customer or project information.

3. QuickBooks Online

The application may connect to QuickBooks Online to retrieve accounting information, including customers, projects, accounts, and transactions, and to assist with recording and reconciling business expenses.

The application is intended for use by authorized users of Lesik Electrical Services LLC.

4. Accuracy and Review

Receipt information may be extracted using automated processing. Users are responsible for reviewing information before accounting transactions are created, modified, or reconciled.

5. Availability

The application is provided for internal business use and may be modified, interrupted, or discontinued at any time.
